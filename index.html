Our Story

Welcome to Madras Tandoori, where we bring the authentic taste of India to Glasgow. Our chefs use traditional cooking methods and the finest spices to create dishes that transport you to the vibrant streets of India.

Every dish is crafted with passion, using family recipes passed down through generations. From our signature tandoori specialties to aromatic curries, we promise an unforgettable dining experience.
